Baby Shower & Godh Bharai Planners in Ranchi: Rituals, Themes, Venues & Décor

A celebration for the mother-to-be that blends a centuries-old ritual with a modern party. Here is how to plan one that honours both

By White Horse Event Management — Event Management Company in Ranchi • October 2026 • 9 min read

The godh bharai is one of the oldest celebrations in an Indian family's calendar. Elders fill the lap of the mother-to-be with fruit, sweets and gifts, bless her, feed her what she craves, and sing. In Ranchi, where families come from Bihar, Bengal, Rajasthan, Punjab and the tribal communities of Jharkhand, the ritual has as many versions as there are kitchens. What has changed in the last few years is what happens around it: a themed backdrop, a dessert table, games, a photographer, and friends invited alongside the family.

White Horse Event Management has planned family celebrations in Ranchi and Jamshedpur for more than ten years. This guide covers how to plan a baby shower or godh bharai that respects the ritual, looks beautiful, and keeps the mother-to-be comfortable: the timing, the formats, the themes, the venue, the menu, the games, the photographs, the timeline and what the cost depends on.

Plan Around the Mother-to-Be

  • • Keep it to two or three hours. A late-pregnancy afternoon is tiring. A short, well-paced function is kinder than a long one.
  • • Daytime, not night. A late-morning or early-afternoon start suits the ritual, the photographs and the guest of honour's energy.
  • • Comfort first. A proper seat with back support, shade or air conditioning, a washroom close by, water within reach, and no long walks or stairs.
  • • Ask her. Some mothers want a traditional ritual with family only; some want friends, music and games. Plan for her, not for the photographs.

The Ritual and Its Regional Versions

A planner's first job is to understand which tradition the family follows, because the décor, the seating and the sequence all flow from it.

Godh bharai (Bihari, UP, Marwari and Punjabi families)

The mother-to-be is seated, usually in a new sari or lehenga, and married women fill her lap with fruit, dry fruit, sweets and coins while singing. A tikka and a small aarti often open it. Décor leans traditional: marigold, banana leaves, a decorated seat or swing.

Saadh or shaad (Bengali families)

A feast for the mother-to-be in the later months, with an elaborate meal of her favourite dishes served on a banana leaf or a decorated thali, and blessings from the elders. The food is the centrepiece, so the menu and the table setting carry the décor.

Valaikappu and seemantham (South Indian families)

Bangles in glass and gold are slipped onto the mother-to-be's wrists by the women of the family, with a puja and a traditional meal. A bangle display and a flower-heavy, jasmine-led décor suit this beautifully.

Dohale jevan and other versions

Maharashtrian families hold a dohale jevan with a floral swing and a feast of the mother's cravings. Many families in Ranchi mix traditions where the couple comes from two communities; we plan one sequence that gives each its place.

Formats That Work in Ranchi

Traditional function at home

Family and close relatives, the ritual in the living room or on the terrace, a decorated seat or swing, a home-cooked or catered lunch. The most common and the most intimate. A backdrop, floral seating and good lighting lift it without changing its character.

Ritual plus themed party

A thirty-minute ritual led by the elders, then a themed celebration with friends: games, a dessert table, a photo corner and lunch. The format most Ranchi families choose now. The trick is to let the ritual finish before the party starts, not to run them at the same time.

Brunch or high tea at a venue

A restaurant private room, a café or a hotel's smaller function room, with a set menu, soft music and a styled table. Suits couples whose friends are the main guest list and families who do not want to host at home.

Garden celebration

A lawn or garden from October to March, with a floral arch, a swing, long tables and a light lunch. Ranchi's winter weather makes this the prettiest option; shade and heaters as the season demands.

Couple's shower

Increasingly, the father-to-be and his friends are part of the celebration rather than waiting outside. Games for both, a joint photo session and a mixed guest list.

Themes and Décor Ideas

Because the sex of the baby is not known before birth in India, themes are neutral by design. That turns out to be a gift: the best baby shower décor is about the mother and the season, not pink or blue.

Traditional marigold and banana leaf

Marigold strings, banana leaves, brass urlis with floating flowers, a decorated wooden seat or jhoola. Fits the ritual perfectly and photographs richly.

Pastel floral

Blush, peach, sage and cream with roses, carnations and baby's breath, a round or arched backdrop, and a floral swing for the mother-to-be. The most requested look in Ranchi right now.

Boho and earthy

Pampas grass, dried florals, rattan, macramé and warm neutrals. Suits garden and café settings and ages well in photographs.

Twinkle twinkle and moon-and-stars

Navy or dusty blue with gold stars, a crescent moon prop, fairy lights and cloud balloons. Works especially well indoors and in the evening.

Jungle, safari and teddy bear

Greens and warm browns with animal cut-outs, leaves and a teddy bear or balloon centrepiece. Child-friendly if there are nieces and nephews on the guest list.

"Oh Baby" balloon and neon

An organic balloon garland in muted tones, a neon sign or acrylic lettering, and a styled dessert table. Modern, quick to set up and strong on social media.

Décor elements that matter most

  • ✅ The seat. A decorated chair, a floral swing or a low traditional seat for the ritual. It must be comfortable, stable and easy to get in and out of.
  • ✅ The backdrop. One strong backdrop behind the seat serves the ritual, the family photographs and the guests' own pictures.
  • ✅ The dessert and gift table. Styled to the theme, with a cake, mithai and a place for gifts and the lap-filling items.
  • ✅ Entrance and welcome. A small welcome sign, a floral arch or a name board sets the tone as guests arrive.
  • ✅ Lighting. Daylight is best; indoors, warm lights and no harsh overheads.

White Horse Events tip: Keep the ritual area and the party area visually connected but physically separate. The elders get a calm space for the blessings, and the games and music do not have to wait for them to finish.

Choosing a Venue in Ranchi

  • ✅ Up to 40 guests: the family home or terrace, with a tent in summer and monsoon and a backdrop and seating arranged by the planner.
  • ✅ 40 to 100 guests: a restaurant private dining room, a café buyout, a club room or a hotel's smaller function space.
  • ✅ 100 and above: a banquet hall, or a lawn between October and March. Our banquet hall guide covers what to check.
  • ✅ Checklist for the mother-to-be: ground-floor access or a lift, a clean washroom close to the seating, air conditioning or shade, and a quiet corner to rest if she needs it.
  • ✅ Season: winter is ideal for gardens; summer and monsoon functions belong indoors. Our winter planning guide explains the Ranchi calendar.

The Menu

In almost every tradition, feeding the mother-to-be what she craves is part of the ritual. Build the menu around that and the rest follows.

  • ✅ Her cravings first. Ask her for five dishes and make sure they are on the table, whether that is litti chokha, phuchka, biryani or a particular mithai.
  • ✅ Light and daytime. A lunch or high-tea menu with chaat, small plates, a few mains and a dessert table suits the timing better than a heavy dinner spread.
  • ✅ Vegetarian and sattvik options for the ritual segment and for elders who prefer them.
  • ✅ Fresh, safe and hygienic. Caterers should be briefed that a pregnant guest of honour and often young children are present; avoid raw and undercooked items and keep food covered and fresh.
  • ✅ A cake and a mithai counter. The cake-cutting is now a standard photo moment; traditional sweets keep the elders happy.
  • ✅ Mocktails and fresh juices instead of alcohol; this is a daytime family function.

Games, Activities and the Moments That Matter

  • ✅ Guess the baby's birth date and weight on a board that the family keeps and checks afterwards.
  • ✅ Advice and wishes cards from every guest, collected into a keepsake box or book for the baby.
  • ✅ Baby photo match: guests bring their own baby photographs and everyone guesses who is who.
  • ✅ Onesie or bib decorating with fabric paints, a quiet activity that gives the family a set of handmade gifts.
  • ✅ The couple's quiz: how well does the father-to-be know the mother-to-be's cravings, due date and nursery plans.
  • ✅ A short song or dance by the cousins, placed early and kept short.
  • ✅ The blessing circle: each elder says one line for the mother and the baby, recorded on video. This is the moment families value most afterwards.

White Horse Events tip: Give the games a host. A cousin or friend with a microphone and a list keeps them moving; without one, games stall and the afternoon drifts.

Gifts, Favours and Photography

Gifts for the mother-to-be

Traditional lap-filling items, jewellery and clothes from the family, and practical gifts from friends. A registry or a shared wish list avoids five of the same thing.

Return favours

Small plants, scented candles, dry-fruit boxes, personalised cookies or a sweet box. Keep them themed and easy to carry.

Photography

A photographer for two to three hours with a shot list: the ritual, the lap-filling, the couple at the backdrop, each family group, the dessert table and candid moments. Daylight makes this easier and cheaper. Our photography guide explains how to brief one.

Maternity shoot

Many couples pair the shower with a maternity shoot, either at the venue before guests arrive or at one of the scenic locations around Ranchi on another day.

A Simple Timeline and What Drives the Cost

  • ✅ 4 to 6 weeks before: fix the date with the elders, decide home or venue, set the guest list and the theme. Book the venue if needed.
  • ✅ 3 weeks before: confirm décor, caterer or menu, cake, photographer. Send invitations.
  • ✅ 2 weeks before: order favours and games material, finalise the mother-to-be's outfit, plan the ritual sequence with the family.
  • ✅ 1 week before: confirm RSVPs and the final count, reconfirm vendors, assign a games host and a family point of contact.
  • ✅ On the day: décor set up two to three hours before guests arrive, the ritual first, then the party, then lunch, and the mother-to-be home and resting by late afternoon.

Cost drivers

  • ✅ Venue: home costs nothing to hire; a restaurant or banquet adds a minimum spend or a hall charge.
  • ✅ Décor: a single backdrop with a seat is the base; a floral swing, fresh-flower installations, balloon garlands and an entrance add to it.
  • ✅ Catering: guest count multiplied by the menu standard.
  • ✅ Photography, cake, favours and games: smaller items that add up, so decide which ones matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Baby Showers & Godh Bharai — Ranchi

When is a godh bharai or baby shower usually held?

+

Most families hold it in the seventh month of pregnancy, and some in the ninth, depending on the family's tradition and the mother's comfort. A daytime function of two to three hours is the most common format.

Can we combine the traditional godh bharai with a modern baby shower?

+

Yes, and most Ranchi families now do. A short ritual segment led by the elders, with the mother-to-be seated and blessed, followed by a themed party with games, food and photographs. The two sit comfortably in one afternoon if the sequence is planned.

Are gender reveal parties done in India?

+

No. Determining the sex of an unborn child is prohibited under the PCPNDT Act, so gender reveal parties are not part of celebrations in India. Baby showers here use neutral themes and colours, and the surprise is the celebration itself.

What venue is best for a baby shower in Ranchi?

+

For up to forty guests, the family home or terrace with a tent and a backdrop. For forty to a hundred, a restaurant private dining room, a café buyout or a small banquet. For larger gatherings, a banquet hall or a lawn in the winter months. Comfort for the mother-to-be matters more than anything else: shade, seating, a nearby washroom and no long walks.
