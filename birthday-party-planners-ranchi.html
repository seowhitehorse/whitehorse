Birthday Parties, Ranchi, 2026 Guide

Birthday Party Planners in Ranchi: Themes, Venues & Costs (2026)

From kids' theme parties to milestone celebrations and surprise parties, everything you need to plan an unforgettable birthday in Ranchi

By White Horse Event Management, Best Event Management Company in Ranchi • August 2026 • 9 min read

Birthdays are no longer just cake and balloons. From themed first birthdays and superhero parties for kids to elegant 25th, 50th, and 60th milestone celebrations, families in Ranchi now want parties that feel personal, look beautiful in photos, and run without stress.

As Ranchi's trusted event management company, White Horse Event Management plans birthdays of every scale, from intimate home celebrations to grand banquet parties. In this guide, we cover the most popular party types, venue options, decoration themes, and what a birthday party really costs in Ranchi in 2026.

Birthday Party Costs in Ranchi at a Glance (2026)

  • • Home decoration setups: ₹5,000 to ₹25,000
  • • Kids' theme parties at cafes and play zones: ₹25,000 to ₹80,000
  • • Banquet hall birthday parties with catering: ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000
  • • Grand milestone celebrations with entertainment: ₹2,50,000 to ₹6,00,000+
  • • Book venues and decorators 3 to 4 weeks ahead, more for weekend dates

1. Kids' Theme Birthday Parties

Theme parties are the heart of kids' birthdays in Ranchi. Popular themes in 2026 include jungle safari, space, unicorns and princesses, superheroes, cricket, and cartoon favourites. A good theme party combines coordinated decor, a themed cake table, games and activities, a host or entertainer, and return gifts.

What it includes: Themed decor and balloon setups, cake table styling, games host, tattoo and craft counters, return gifts

Indicative cost: ₹25,000 to ₹80,000 at a cafe, play zone, or lawn

Best for: Birthdays for ages 1 to 12, especially first birthdays and school-friend parties.

2. Milestone Birthdays: 18th, 25th, 50th, and 60th

Milestone birthdays call for a more elegant celebration: think curated decor palettes, live food counters, a photo wall, music or a live band, and an anchor to run the evening. Sixtieth birthdays with a shashti poorti touch and surprise golden-jubilee parties for parents are increasingly popular in Ranchi.

What it includes: Elegant decor, catering with live counters, entertainment, photography, anchor and AV setup

Indicative cost: ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000 at a banquet hall; grand celebrations ₹2,50,000 to ₹6,00,000+

Best for: Adult milestone birthdays and family celebrations where guests span generations.

3. Surprise Parties and Intimate Home Celebrations

Not every birthday needs a hall. Home terrace parties, room decoration surprises, candlelight dinners, and small gatherings of 15 to 40 guests are a big trend. Professional decorators transform a living room or terrace in two to three hours with balloon arches, fairy lights, neon signs, and personalised backdrops.

What it includes: Balloon and backdrop decor, cake and dessert table, optional catering and photographer

Indicative cost: ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 for decor; ₹25,000 to ₹60,000 with catering and photography

Best for: Surprise parties, anniversaries-style intimate birthdays, and last-minute celebrations.

4. Where to Host a Birthday Party in Ranchi

Ranchi offers venues for every party size and budget: cafes and restaurants with private sections, kids' play zones, rooftop lounges, hotel banquet halls, and open lawns for larger evening parties. The right choice depends on guest count, season, and whether you want in-house or outside catering.

  • • Cafes and restaurants: best for 20 to 60 guests, minimal setup needed
  • • Play zones and activity centres: best for kids' parties with built-in entertainment
  • • Banquet halls and hotels: best for 100+ guests with catering and parking
  • • Lawns and rooftops: best for winter evening parties with open-air decor
