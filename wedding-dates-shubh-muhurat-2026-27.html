Wedding Dates & Shubh Muhurat 2026-27

When to get married this season — auspicious months, dates to avoid, and why you must book venues early

By White Horse Events • September 2026 • 9 min read

In Hindu tradition, weddings are held only on shubh muhurat — auspicious dates and times determined by the panchang. That is why lakhs of weddings across India cluster into a few peak months, and why venues, caterers and photographers in Ranchi and Jamshedpur get booked out months in advance.

Here is your complete guide to the 2026-27 wedding season — when the auspicious windows fall, which periods to avoid, and how to plan around them.

The 2026-27 Wedding Season at a Glance

  • ✓ November 2026: The season opens after Dev Uthani Ekadashi (late November) — the single most popular wedding window in North India
  • ✓ Early–mid December 2026: Strong muhurat dates before Kharmas begins
  • ✓ Mid-December to mid-January: Kharmas — traditionally no weddings are held in this period
  • ✓ Late January – February 2027: The biggest stretch of muhurat dates, including the Basant Panchami period, considered auspicious for weddings
  • ✓ April – June 2027: The summer window (Akshaya Tritiya in April/May is considered an 'abujh' muhurat — auspicious without consulting the panchang)
  • ✓ July – October 2027: Chaturmas — the wedding season pauses until Dev Uthani Ekadashi

Important: Confirm Your Exact Date with the Panchang

Muhurat dates vary by panchang, region and the couple's kundli. Treat this guide as your season planner, and confirm the final tithi and lagna with your family pandit before printing cards. Once your date is locked, share it with us — we block your venue and vendors the same week.

Why Early Booking Matters So Much

On a single big muhurat date, Ranchi alone can host hundreds of weddings. That means banquet halls, marriage gardens, caterers, decorators and photographers are all fighting over the same few dates.

  • ✓ 6–8 months before: finalise date, book venue and wedding planner
  • ✓ 4–6 months before: book photographer, caterer, decor theme and DJ or band
  • ✓ 2–3 months before: outfits, invitations, guest list and logistics
  • ✓ Final month: tastings, timelines and function-wise run-throughs

Getting Married on a Popular Muhurat? Here's Our Advice

If your pandit has given you one of the big shared dates, book everything immediately — prices rise and availability vanishes as the date approaches. If you have flexibility, consider a slightly less crowded muhurat in the same fortnight: you will get better venues, better vendor attention and often better rates. Our team tracks availability across Ranchi and Jamshedpur and can tell you exactly which of your shortlisted dates gives you the best options — see our full wedding cost guide for season-wise pricing.

Month-by-Month Season Planner

November 2026 — The Grand Opening

The season begins after Dev Uthani Ekadashi in late November. These first dates are the most crowded of the entire season — if your family is set on a November wedding, your venue should already be booked by June-July.

December 2026 — The Short Window

Strong muhurat dates fall in early-to-mid December before Kharmas begins around December 16. A popular choice for NRI and outstation families combining the wedding with year-end holidays.

Late January – February 2027 — The Big Stretch

The longest run of auspicious dates, including the Basant Panchami period. Ranchi's weather is at its absolute best — perfect for open-lawn weddings. This is our busiest stretch every year.

April – June 2027 — The Summer Window

Akshaya Tritiya anchors this window. Venues are easier to get and rates are softer, but plan AC banquets or evening-only outdoor functions — see our garden vs banquet guide.

Why Prices Surge on Big Muhurat Dates

On a single major muhurat, hundreds of weddings happen across Ranchi and Jamshedpur simultaneously. Every decorator, caterer, photographer and band is triple-booked, and quotes rise 20-40% versus a quieter date in the same fortnight. Demand also strains quality — a decorator running four sites in one night cannot give any of them full attention.

  • ✓ Weekday advantage: a Tuesday muhurat can cost 15-25% less than the Saturday one in the same week, with better vendor availability
  • ✓ Adjacent-date advantage: if your pandit offers multiple dates, the less famous one usually gets you better venues and sharper vendor focus
  • ✓ Off-window advantage: April-June dates offer the season's best value for families with flexibility

Choosing Between Two Dates: A Practical Framework

When the panchang gives your family more than one valid date, compare them on these five practical axes before deciding:

  • ✓ Venue availability: is your preferred venue free on both? (We check this across Ranchi and Jamshedpur within a day)
  • ✓ Weather: a February lawn wedding beats a June one; a December date must dodge the Kharmas boundary
  • ✓ Guest travel: dates adjacent to weekends and holidays dramatically improve outstation attendance
  • ✓ Vendor quality: on quieter dates, the best photographers and decorators are actually available — not just their B-teams
  • ✓ Budget: the same wedding can differ by ₹1-2 lakh between a peak Saturday and a mid-week muhurat

Wedding Dates & Muhurat 2026-27

When does the 2026-27 wedding season start?

+

The main season opens after Dev Uthani Ekadashi in late November 2026, runs till mid-December, pauses for Kharmas, and resumes from late January through February 2027, with a summer window from April to June 2027.

What is Kharmas and why are there no weddings then?

+

Kharmas is the roughly month-long period from mid-December to mid-January when the Sun transits Sagittarius. Auspicious ceremonies including weddings are traditionally not performed during this time.

Which is the most auspicious wedding date in 2027?

+

Akshaya Tritiya (April/May 2027) is considered an abujh muhurat — auspicious for weddings without consulting the panchang. Exact dates for your wedding should always be confirmed with your family pandit.

How early should I book a venue for a 2026-27 muhurat date?

+

At least 6 to 8 months in advance. On big shared muhurat dates, hundreds of weddings happen in Ranchi and Jamshedpur alone, and the best venues go first.

Can White Horse help if my wedding date is very close?

+

Are weekday muhurat dates cheaper than weekend ones?

+

Usually yes — venues and vendors quote 15-25% less on weekday muhurats, and the best teams are more available. The tradeoff is slightly lower outstation guest attendance.

What does 'abujh muhurat' mean?

+

An abujh muhurat, like Akshaya Tritiya, is considered auspicious for weddings without needing individual panchang consultation — which is why lakhs of weddings happen on it.

Why do different panchangs show slightly different muhurat dates?

+

Regional traditions and calculation methods differ. That is why we recommend confirming your final date and lagna with your own family pandit, then locking vendors immediately.
