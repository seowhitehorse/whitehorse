Wedding Checklist Timeline: 12 Months to 1 Week Before Your Big Day in Ranchi

Know exactly what to book, confirm, and finalise — and when — so nothing slips through the cracks

By White Horse Event Management — Best Event Management Company in Ranchi • September 2026 • 11 min read

Planning a wedding in Ranchi is exciting — and overwhelming. The biggest mistake most couples make is not starting too late, but not having a clear sequence. They know they need to book a venue and a caterer, but they are not sure what comes first, what can wait, and what will cost them if they miss the window.

This month-by-month wedding checklist was built from years of planning weddings across Ranchi and Jharkhand. Use it as your master reference — from the first decision to the final morning.

How to Use This Checklist

  • • Work backwards from your wedding date to map out each phase
  • • The earlier you book, the more negotiating power and choice you have
  • • Peak wedding season in Ranchi runs October to February — book venues and caterers at least 6 months early during this period
  • • Use this alongside our 2026 wedding cost guide to budget each phase

12 Months Before: The Foundations

This is where you set the parameters everything else builds on. Decisions made now determine your options later.

  • ✅ Set your overall wedding budget with family
  • ✅ Create a preliminary guest list to understand your scale
  • ✅ Choose your wedding date (check muhurat calendars, local holidays)
  • ✅ Start researching and visiting wedding venues in Ranchi
  • ✅ Book your venue — this is the single most time-sensitive booking
  • ✅ Shortlist and meet wedding planners if hiring one
  • ✅ Begin researching wedding photographers and videographers

Why venues go first: Good banquet halls and lawns in Ranchi are booked 8–12 months ahead for peak season dates. Losing your preferred venue forces a date change or a compromise.

9–10 Months Before: Vendors & Vision

With your venue locked, you now have a canvas. Use this phase to secure your core vendors and establish your wedding aesthetic.

  • ✅ Book your photographer and videographer — top ones in Ranchi fill up fast
  • ✅ Begin caterer shortlisting and menu tastings
  • ✅ Start bridal and groom outfit research
  • ✅ Decide on your wedding theme and colour palette
  • ✅ Research and shortlist decoration vendors
  • ✅ Book your makeup artist, especially for peak season
  • ✅ Begin planning pre-wedding events (sangeet, mehndi, haldi)

6–8 Months Before: Confirmations

This is the most active booking phase. Everything important should be contracted by the end of this window.

  • ✅ Finalise and sign caterer contract with menu and per-plate costs
  • ✅ Book decoration and lighting vendor — share your theme brief
  • ✅ Send save-the-dates to outstation guests
  • ✅ Finalise bridal lehenga / outfit and place order (tailoring takes 3–5 months)
  • ✅ Book DJ and entertainment
  • ✅ Arrange accommodation for out-of-town guests
  • ✅ Plan transportation and logistics for the wedding day
  • ✅ Book pandit / priest and confirm ceremony rituals

3–5 Months Before: Details & Design

The broad strokes are done. Now you refine, personalise, and start managing guest logistics.

  • ✅ Send formal wedding invitations
  • ✅ Finalise decoration concept and mood board with vendor
  • ✅ Order wedding jewellery
  • ✅ Plan honeymoon and make bookings
  • ✅ Finalise guest list and collect RSVPs
  • ✅ Plan and book sangeet, mehndi, and haldi venues and vendors
  • ✅ Order wedding favours and gifts
  • ✅ Confirm photography shot list with your photographer

1–2 Months Before: Final Confirmations

  • ✅ Confirm final guest count with caterer
  • ✅ Final outfit fitting for bride, groom, and close family
  • ✅ Confirm all vendor bookings with written confirmations
  • ✅ Create a master wedding-day schedule and share with vendors
  • ✅ Arrange mehendi and bridal prep timeline
  • ✅ Confirm transport and driver details
  • ✅ Finalise seating arrangement plan
  • ✅ Prepare emergency kit (safety pins, pain relief, phone chargers, etc.)

1 Week Before: The Final Push

This week is about confirming, not deciding. Everything should already be booked. Your job now is to make sure everyone knows the plan.

  • ✅ Call every vendor to reconfirm arrival times and setup details
  • ✅ Share the master schedule with family members who have roles
  • ✅ Collect all vendor contact numbers in one place
  • ✅ Do a venue walkthrough with your planner or coordinator
  • ✅ Pack your wedding outfit, accessories, and emergency kit
  • ✅ Rest, eat well, and enjoy your pre-wedding rituals fully

White Horse Events tip: The week before your wedding should be calm, not chaotic. If you find yourself making frantic calls to vendors this week, it is a sign the earlier phases needed more structure. That is exactly what a good wedding planner handles for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Wedding Checklist & Timeline — Ranchi

How early should I start planning a wedding in Ranchi?

+

For a peak-season wedding (October to February) in Ranchi, start planning at least 10–12 months ahead. For off-season dates, 6–8 months is usually sufficient, though earlier is always better for top venues and vendors.

What should I book first for my wedding in Ranchi?

+

Book your venue first — everything else depends on it. Once your venue is confirmed, book your photographer and caterer next, as good ones fill up quickly during the wedding season.

When should I send wedding invitations in India?

+

Send save-the-dates to outstation guests 4–6 months before and formal invitations 4–6 weeks before the wedding date. For guests travelling from abroad, save-the-dates should go out even earlier.

How long before the wedding should I order a bridal lehenga?

+

Order your bridal lehenga at least 4–6 months before the wedding. Custom and designer outfits take 3–5 months to complete, and you need time for fittings and any alterations.
