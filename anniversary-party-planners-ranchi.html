Anniversary Party Planning in Ranchi: 25th, 50th & Milestone Celebration Ideas

A milestone anniversary is a wedding told in reverse: the couple already knows the ending. Here is how to celebrate it properly

By White Horse Event Management — Event Management Company in Ranchi • October 2026 • 8 min read

Most anniversary celebrations in Ranchi are planned by the children. Parents reaching twenty-five or fifty years together rarely organise their own party, and the people organising it have usually never planned an event of this size before. They want it to feel personal, they want the parents to be surprised or at least moved, and they want it done without the stress of a wedding.

White Horse Event Management has planned celebrations in Ranchi and Jamshedpur for over ten years, from intimate family dinners to large milestone functions. This guide covers the decisions that matter: the format, the venue, the theme, the moments that make it personal, and a timeline that keeps the whole thing manageable.

Start With Three Questions

  • • Surprise or planned? A surprise needs a cover story and a smaller circle of people who know. A planned event lets the couple shape it.
  • • Intimate or everyone? Forty close family members and a hundred and fifty guests are two completely different events.
  • • What does the couple actually enjoy? A couple who love music want a sangeet-style evening. A couple who love their garden want a lunch under the trees. Plan for them, not for the guests.

Anniversary Formats That Work Well in Ranchi

Vow renewal with a small ceremony

A short ceremony, often with a pandit or simply with the children officiating, where the couple exchange garlands or rings again. A decorated mandap-style setting or a floral arch, the original wedding song, and family readings. This is the format that produces the most tears, in a good way.

Family dinner with a programme

A banquet or restaurant private dining room, a sit-down or buffet dinner, and a programme of speeches, a photo film and perhaps a performance by the grandchildren. The most common choice for silver jubilees.

Themed evening or sangeet-style party

Recreate the decade the couple married in, with music, dress code and décor to match. Live band or DJ, choreographed performances by family, and dancing. Works well for couples who had a quiet wedding and want the celebration they never had.

Garden lunch or high tea

A daytime gathering on a lawn or at home, light décor, live acoustic music, a long table lunch. Suits Ranchi's winter weather and older guests who prefer not to be out late.

Destination anniversary

A weekend away for the extended family, within Jharkhand or further afield, with one main celebration dinner. Our destination wedding guide for Jharkhand covers the locations that work for this too.

Ideas by Milestone

25th: Silver Jubilee

Silver and white palette with soft florals, a 25-year photo timeline displayed as a gallery wall, a cake with the original wedding date, and a letter from each child read aloud. The couple is often still working and socially active, so a larger guest list is natural.

50th: Golden Jubilee

Gold and ivory, marigold and yellow rose décor, a recreation of the wedding photograph with the same pose, a short film with messages from relatives who cannot travel, and a seated format with comfortable timings. Keep the programme under two hours.

10th and 15th

Often planned by the couple themselves. A party with friends, a theme, a DJ, a dinner. Décor can be bolder and the evening can run later.

40th and 60th

Ruby and diamond themes respectively. These are family-first occasions. A vow renewal and a lunch with the full extended family is usually exactly right.

Choosing a Venue in Ranchi

Anniversaries do not need wedding-sized venues. Match the space to the guest count so the room feels full and warm, not sparse.

  • ✅ Up to 40 guests: a private dining room at a hotel or restaurant, or the family home with a tent on the terrace or lawn
  • ✅ 40 to 120 guests: a small banquet hall or a hotel's smaller function room, with a stage for the programme
  • ✅ 120 and above: a full banquet hall or a marriage lawn in winter. See our guides to banquet halls and marriage gardens in Ranchi
  • ✅ For older guests: ground-floor access or a lift, comfortable seating for everyone, and the programme early in the evening

The Moments That Make It Personal

Décor and food make an anniversary pleasant. These are the things that make it memorable.

  • ✅ The photo film. Scanned wedding photographs, pictures from each decade, and video messages from friends and family who cannot attend. Three to five minutes, set to songs the couple love. Start collecting photographs early; this takes longer than anything else.
  • ✅ Recreating the wedding photograph. Same pose, same people where possible, fifty years on.
  • ✅ The original invitation card reproduced as the anniversary invitation, or displayed at the entrance.
  • ✅ Speeches with a time limit. One from the children, one from a sibling or oldest friend, one from the couple. Three good speeches beat ten long ones.
  • ✅ A guest book or message wall where every guest writes a line, collected into an album afterwards.
  • ✅ Their song. Played live if you can, for the couple's first dance or simply as they enter.
  • ✅ Grandchildren's performance. Short, rehearsed, and placed early before the youngest ones tire.

White Horse Events tip: Appoint one family member as the only person the planner and vendors call on the day. The children organising the event should be at the party, not on the phone.

A Simple Planning Timeline

  • ✅ 8 to 10 weeks before: Fix the date, format and guest count. Book the venue. Start collecting photographs and video messages.
  • ✅ 6 weeks before: Confirm caterer and menu, décor theme, entertainment. Send invitations.
  • ✅ 4 weeks before: Order the cake and gifts. Brief the photographer. Finalise the programme and speakers.
  • ✅ 2 weeks before: Finish the photo film. Confirm RSVPs and share the final count with the caterer.
  • ✅ 1 week before: Reconfirm every vendor. Walk through the venue. Rehearse the children's performance and the speeches.
  • ✅ On the day: Décor and AV set-up in the morning, sound check with the film and music, family arrives early, couple arrives last.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anniversary Celebrations — Ranchi

How much time is needed to plan a 25th or 50th anniversary party in Ranchi?

+

Eight to ten weeks is comfortable for a celebration of up to a few hundred guests. The photo film and messages from relatives usually take the longest, so start collecting those first.

What is a good venue for an anniversary celebration in Ranchi?

+

For under forty guests, a private dining room or the family home. For forty to a hundred and twenty, a small banquet hall or hotel function room. For larger gatherings, a full banquet hall or a marriage lawn in the winter months.

What are good themes for a silver and golden jubilee?

+

Silver and white with soft florals for the 25th, gold and ivory with marigold and yellow roses for the 50th. Recreating the decade of the original wedding in music, dress code and décor is also popular.

Can we organise a surprise anniversary party?

+

Yes. It needs a believable cover story, a small group who know the plan, and a planner who coordinates vendors without involving the couple. Keep the surprise element to the arrival and let the couple enjoy the rest openly.
