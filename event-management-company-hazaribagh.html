Event Management Company in Hazaribagh: Weddings, Corporate Events & Celebrations

Two hours from Ranchi, with its own venues, seasons and traditions. Here is how White Horse Events plans and runs events in Hazaribagh

By White Horse Event Management — Event Management Company in Ranchi • November 2026 • 9 min read

Hazaribagh sits about ninety kilometres north of Ranchi on the national highway, a district headquarters with a university, a cantonment, a lake and a hill at its centre, and a wide rural hinterland of coal, agriculture and small industry around it. Its families host weddings at the same scale as the capital, often with relatives arriving from Ranchi, Patna and Kolkata, and its institutions, banks, dealerships and public sector units hold their annual days, conferences and felicitations through the year. What the town has little of is full-service event management: companies that design, budget, contract and run an event end to end rather than only supply a tent or a stage.

White Horse Event Management has planned events across Jharkhand for more than ten years, with over 500 events delivered, a head office on Mahatma Gandhi Main Road in Ranchi and a second office in Bistupur, Jamshedpur. This page explains how we work in Hazaribagh, what we handle, which venue types suit which events, what the cost looks like and how the timeline runs when your planner is two hours down the highway.

Hazaribagh at a Glance for Event Planning

  • • From Ranchi: roughly 90 km by road via Ramgarh, about two hours. Hazaribagh Town railway station connects towards Ranchi and Koderma, and Ranchi is the nearest airport.
  • • Event areas: Matwari, Jhanda Chowk, Korra and the main road corridor in town; Barhi and Ichak in the district; and Ramgarh, which many families treat as a venue option between the two cities.
  • • Landmarks for shoots and small gatherings: Hazaribagh Lake and Canary Hill in town, with the wildlife sanctuary and forest roads nearby for pre-wedding shoots, subject to permissions.
  • • Season: the wedding calendar runs November to February with a second window in late April to June; the town is slightly cooler than Ranchi in winter, so heating matters for lawn events.

Venue Types in Hazaribagh and What They Suit

Hotel banquet halls

Air-conditioned halls with in-house catering and rooms for outstation guests. Best for receptions of 150 to 300, corporate dinners and monsoon-season functions. The number of such halls in Hazaribagh is small, so book early.

Marriage halls and community halls

The mainstay for weddings in town: large, affordable and flexible on outside caterers. Décor, lighting and sound are brought in and designed, which is where a planner transforms the space.

Marriage gardens and lawns

The winter favourite for large weddings and baraat receptions, in town and along the highway towards Ramgarh. Open-air mandap, tented dining, room for a stage and dance floor. Our marriage garden guide lists what to check.

Auditoriums and institutional grounds

The university, colleges, the cantonment and public sector townships have auditoriums and grounds that suit annual days, conferences and felicitations, with permissions through the institution.

Home and terrace functions

Tilak, haldi, mehndi and small celebrations are mostly held at home. A tent, a backdrop, seating and a compact sound set-up turn a courtyard or terrace into a function space. See home décor ideas for haldi and mehndi.

Ranchi as the alternative

For a large reception or a destination-style wedding with hotel rooms for everyone, many Hazaribagh families choose Ranchi, two hours away. Our Ranchi wedding venue guide covers the options.
