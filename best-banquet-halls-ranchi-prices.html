Best Banquet Halls in Ranchi with Prices: A 2026 Guide

Compare AC banquet halls, marriage halls, and party halls in Ranchi by capacity, budget, and location

By White Horse Event Management, Best Event Management Company in Ranchi • July 2026 • 9 min read

Choosing the right banquet hall is one of the most important decisions for any wedding, reception, or celebration in Ranchi. The hall shapes your guest experience, decides how many people you can invite, and influences your catering and decoration options.

Ranchi has a wide choice of banquet halls, from affordable community halls to premium air conditioned venues with in-house catering. As Ranchi's trusted event management company, White Horse Event Management helps couples and families shortlist and book the best banquet hall for their needs. In this guide, we explain the main options with indicative 2026 price ranges.

Banquet Hall Prices in Ranchi at a Glance (2026)

  • • Budget community and marriage halls: ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000 (hall rental)
  • • Mid-range AC banquet halls: ₹1,50,000 to ₹4,00,000
  • • Premium hotel banquets: often ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 per plate
  • • Prices rise sharply during peak wedding season (October to February)

1. Budget Banquet and Marriage Halls in Ranchi

Budget halls are ideal for families who want a comfortable, functional space for a traditional wedding or reception without a premium price tag. These halls usually charge a flat rental and allow outside catering and decorators.

Typical capacity: 150 to 500 guests

Indicative price: ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000 (hall rental)

Best for: Traditional weddings, receptions, and family functions on a controlled budget.

2. Mid-Range AC Banquet Halls

Air conditioned banquet halls offer a more comfortable experience with better interiors, parking, and sometimes in-house catering. They are the most popular choice for weddings in Ranchi because they balance comfort and cost.

Typical capacity: 300 to 800 guests

Indicative price: ₹1,50,000 to ₹4,00,000, or per plate packages

Best for: Mid-size weddings and receptions that want AC comfort and modern amenities.

3. Premium Hotel Banquet Halls

For grand weddings and high-profile receptions, Ranchi's premium hotels offer elegant banquet halls with five-star hospitality, in-house catering, and luxury accommodation for guests. These venues are usually priced per plate.

Typical capacity: 300 to 1,500 guests

Indicative price: ₹1,500 to ₹3,500 per plate

Best for: Luxury weddings, corporate galas, and celebrations that want a premium, fully managed experience.

For a detailed list of top hotels and resorts, read our guide to the best wedding venues in Ranchi and Jharkhand.

What to Check Before Booking a Banquet Hall in Ranchi

  • • Capacity, so the hall comfortably fits your guest count with dining and stage space
  • • Catering policy, whether in-house catering is required or outside caterers are allowed
  • • Air conditioning, power backup, and clean, adequate washrooms
  • • Parking space and easy access for your guests
  • • Decor rules and any restrictions on setup and timings
  • • Total cost, including taxes, so there are no surprises later
