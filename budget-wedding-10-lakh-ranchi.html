How to Plan a Wedding in ₹10 Lakh in Ranchi

A realistic, function-by-function budget breakdown — and the honest truth about where to save and where to spend

By White Horse Events • September 2026 • 10 min read

Can you really have a beautiful wedding in Ranchi for ₹10 lakh? Yes — if you plan smart, prioritise ruthlessly and book early. We have planned dozens of weddings in this range. Here is exactly how the budget works, based on real Ranchi prices. (For the full market picture across all budgets, see our wedding cost in Ranchi guide.)

The ₹10 Lakh Breakdown (250-300 Guests)

  • • Venue (1 day): ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,50,000 — marriage garden or mid-range banquet
  • • Catering (300 guests × ₹450-550): ₹1,50,000 – ₹1,80,000
  • • Decor & mandap: ₹1,20,000 – ₹1,50,000
  • • Photography & videography: ₹80,000 – ₹1,00,000
  • • Bridal outfits & makeup: ₹1,00,000 – ₹1,30,000
  • • Groom's outfits & grooming: ₹40,000 – ₹60,000
  • • DJ / band / baraat: ₹50,000 – ₹70,000
  • • Invitations & gifting: ₹40,000 – ₹60,000
  • • Pandit, transport & misc: ₹50,000 – ₹70,000
  • • Contingency (always keep this): ₹80,000 – ₹1,00,000

Total: roughly ₹9.1 – ₹10.7 lakh. Note this covers the wedding day and one combined haldi-mehndi function at home; jewellery is extra and family-specific.

Where to Save (Without Anyone Noticing)

  • ✓ Date: a non-peak muhurat or a weekday can cut venue costs 20-30% — check our 2026-27 muhurat guide
  • ✓ Venue: marriage gardens and lawns cost far less than five-star banquets and look stunning with good lighting
  • ✓ Decor: genda phool, fairy lights and drapes photograph beautifully at a fraction of exotic-flower costs
  • ✓ Functions: combine haldi and mehndi into one at-home function — see our home decoration ideas
  • ✓ Invitations: a beautiful digital invite for most guests, printed cards only for elders

Where NOT to Cut

  • ✓ Catering quality: guests forget the decor, never the food — this is the last place to economise
  • ✓ Photography: these are the only things you keep forever; a weak photographer is a permanent regret
  • ✓ Power backup & sound: cheap generators and speakers fail exactly when it matters

The Honest Planner's Tip

The single biggest budget killer is late booking — prices for everything rise 20-40% in the final 2 months before peak-season dates. Book your venue and core vendors 6+ months out and the ₹10 lakh plan becomes genuinely comfortable.

The Function-by-Function Plan

Haldi + Mehndi (at home) — ₹60,000-80,000

One combined daytime function at home: marigold decor (₹20-25k), home-style catering for 80-100 (₹30-35k), dhol and music (₹8-10k). Our home decoration guide shows exactly how to make this look designer-done.

Wedding Day (garden venue) — ₹7.5-8.5 lakh

The main event carries the budget: venue, catering for 300, mandap and stage decor, photography, baraat and the pheras. Everything in the breakdown above concentrates here.

Reception — combine it

The honest ₹10-lakh advice: hold the reception dinner on the wedding night itself at the same venue. A separate reception day adds ₹2.5-4 lakh minimum; combining saves the venue, decor and a second catering minimum.

5 Negotiation Tips That Actually Work

  • ✓ Book the venue for a weekday or smaller muhurat — then use the saving as your decor budget; see which dates cost less
  • ✓ Ask caterers for a per-plate rate at 3 quality tiers — the ₹450 plate done well beats the ₹600 plate done badly, and tasting tells you which caterer that is
  • ✓ Negotiate decor by element, not package — 'mandap + stage + gate + 20 table arrangements' priced separately always comes out cheaper than 'wedding decor package'
  • ✓ Pay photographers for deliverables, not hours — fix the album, edited-photo count and film length in writing
  • ✓ One advance, milestone payments — never pay any vendor more than 30% upfront; balance against delivery milestones

₹10 Lakh vs ₹15 Lakh: What the Extra 5 Buys

  • • +₹1.5 lakh: a premium garden or hotel banquet instead of a standard lawn
  • • +₹1 lakh: catering upgrade — live counters, one more cuisine, premium desserts
  • • +₹1 lakh: theme decor with premium florals instead of standard mandap-and-stage
  • • +₹75,000: cinematic wedding film with drone, plus a separate reception-night photographer
  • • +₹75,000: a separate sangeet night with DJ, dance floor and choreography

If those upgrades matter to your family, ₹15 lakh is the honest number. If they don't, ₹10 lakh done smartly will look and feel complete — and nobody at your wedding will be able to tell what it cost.

Common Budget Mistakes We See Every Season

  • ✓ Booking late and paying panic prices for whatever is left
  • ✓ Padding the guest list 'to be safe' — 50 phantom guests cost ₹25,000+ in catering alone
  • ✓ Spending on 20 small decor spots instead of 4 high-impact ones (gate, mandap, stage, couple entry)
  • ✓ No contingency line — something always comes up; the 10% buffer is what keeps it from becoming debt
  • ✓ Skipping written itemised quotes — verbal 'sab included hai' promises are where budgets die

Frequently Asked Questions

Budget Weddings in Ranchi

Is ₹10 lakh enough for a wedding in Ranchi?

+

Yes — for a wedding of 250-300 guests with a good venue, quality catering, tasteful decor and professional photography, ₹10 lakh is a realistic and comfortable budget in Ranchi if you book early.

What is the biggest expense in a Ranchi wedding?

+

Venue plus catering together typically take 35-40% of the total budget, followed by decor and outfits.

How can I reduce my wedding budget without it looking cheap?

+

Choose a non-peak date, pick a marriage garden over a five-star banquet, use marigold-and-lights decor, combine pre-wedding functions and go digital-first on invitations.

Do wedding planners make sense for budget weddings?

+

Yes — often more than for luxury weddings. A planner's vendor rates, negotiation and mistake-prevention typically save more than the planning fee costs.

Can White Horse plan a wedding under ₹10 lakh?

+

Yes. We plan weddings across budgets and will give you a transparent function-by-function quotation. Call +91-94315-81052 for a free consultation.

Should we have a separate reception on a ₹10 lakh budget?

+

Combine the reception dinner with the wedding night at the same venue — a separate reception day adds ₹2.5-4 lakh minimum. On this budget, one grand evening beats two stretched ones.

How much advance should we pay vendors?

+

Never more than 30% upfront, with the balance tied to delivery milestones. Get every inclusion in writing before paying anything.

What is the single best way to cut wedding costs in Ranchi?

+

Choose a weekday or less-crowded muhurat date. It cuts venue and vendor costs 15-25% across the board — the same wedding, simply on a smarter date.
