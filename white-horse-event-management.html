White Horse Event Management: Who We Are & What We Do

The official guide to White Horse Events: our story, our services, our two offices and how to reach the right person

Call +91-94315-81052 WhatsApp Us

If you have searched for White Horse Event Management, White Horse Events or simply White Horse Event, you are in the right place. They are all the same company: a full-service event management firm headquartered in Ranchi, Jharkhand, with a second office in Jamshedpur.

This page is the quickest way to understand who we are, what we do, which office to call, and what to expect when you work with us.

Our Story

White Horse Event Management was founded by Taranbir Singh Sahni, our Founder & Creative Director, with a simple idea: celebrations in Jharkhand deserved the same design and execution standards as those in India's metro cities, delivered by a team that actually lives here and knows the venues, vendors and traditions.

Over 10+ years we have grown from a Ranchi wedding team into a company that has delivered more than 500 events, opened a second office in Bistupur, Jamshedpur, and planned destination weddings for Jharkhand families in Goa, Udaipur, Jaipur and beyond. Read more about what sets White Horse apart in Ranchi.

What White Horse Events Does

Weddings

Complete wedding planning: venue, mandap and stage design, decor, catering coordination, sangeet, mehndi, haldi, baraat, reception and guest hospitality. See our wedding planner in Ranchi and wedding planner in Jamshedpur pages.

Corporate events

Conferences, product launches, annual days, dealer meets, award nights and township events with full production. Details on corporate event management.

Destination weddings

Planned from Ranchi or Jamshedpur for locations across India and scenic spots within Jharkhand. See destination wedding planning.

Birthdays, engagements & private celebrations

Theme birthdays, ring ceremonies, anniversaries and home functions with decor, catering and entertainment sized to the occasion.

Decor, production & entertainment

Stage, lighting, florals, LED, sound, DJ, band and artist management, available as standalone services.

How We Work

1. Free consultation

At either office, on a call or over WhatsApp. We discuss the occasion, date, guest count, venue preference and budget, and give you an honest read on what is achievable.

2. Proposal & itemised quotation

Venue options, decor concept, menu direction and a function-by-function quotation with every line visible.

3. Booking & a single coordinator

Once confirmed, we lock venue and vendors, and one coordinator becomes your point of contact until the event is over.

4. Execution

Our team is on site before the first guest and stays until pack-down, running the timeline and every vendor.
