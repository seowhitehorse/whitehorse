Top 10 Pre-Wedding Shoot Locations in Ranchi

From Patratu Valley's winding roads to hidden waterfalls — where Ranchi couples get their dream shoots

By White Horse Events • September 2026 • 10 min read

Ranchi is one of the most underrated pre-wedding shoot destinations in India — waterfalls, valleys, lakes and hills all within a 1-2 hour drive of the city. Here are the ten locations our photography teams shoot at most, with the best time for each.

The 10 Best Locations

1. Patratu Valley (40 km)

The iconic winding-road viewpoint and the dam-side ghats make this Ranchi's most famous shoot location. Golden hour here is magical — reach by 4 PM for the best light.

2. Rock Garden & Kanke Dam

Right inside the city — sculpted rocks, lake views and sunset points. Perfect for a quick half-day shoot on a budget.

3. Dassam Falls (40 km)

A dramatic 144-ft waterfall on the Kanchi river. Best from October to February when the flow is strong but safe to approach.

4. Hundru Falls (45 km)

Jharkhand's most spectacular waterfall at 320 ft. The rocky terrain gives bold, cinematic frames — carry comfortable footwear for the steps.

5. Jonha Falls (40 km)

Quieter than Dassam and Hundru, with a beautiful stepped approach — great for couples who want privacy during the shoot.

6. Tagore Hill

Heritage stone structures and a panoramic city view — lovely for sunrise shoots and traditional-outfit frames.

7. Nakshatra Van

A landscaped garden opposite Raj Bhavan with fountains and flower beds — easy, comfortable and great for family-approved classic shots.

8. Ranchi Lake (Bada Talab)

Boats, water reflections and the Swami Vivekananda statue backdrop — best in early morning light.

9. Getalsud Dam (35 km)

Wide open water and big-sky sunsets — a favourite for drone shots and silhouette frames.

10. Netarhat (150 km) — the bonus trip

The 'Queen of Chotanagpur'. If you can make it an overnight trip, the sunrise and sunset points here rival any hill station shoot in India.

Best Season for Pre-Wedding Shoots in Ranchi

October to February is ideal — waterfalls are full, skies are clear and outdoor shoots are comfortable all day. Monsoon (July-September) gives lush green frames but plan indoor backups. Avoid peak summer afternoons.

Practical Tips Before You Shoot

  • ✓ Permits: some locations charge small entry/camera fees — your photographer or planner should arrange these in advance
  • ✓ Timing: golden hour (first and last 90 minutes of daylight) gives the best frames at every location on this list
  • ✓ Outfits: plan 2-3 changes and pick locations that offer changing access — or shoot city locations and valley locations on separate days
  • ✓ Safety: at waterfalls, stay on marked paths — especially just after monsoon
  • ✓ Full package: we coordinate the photographer, travel, permits, outfits and makeup as part of our wedding planning packages

A Sample 2-Day Shoot Plan

Day 1 — City Day (Half Day)

Morning at Tagore Hill for sunrise heritage frames, then Nakshatra Van for garden shots, ending at Ranchi Lake before noon. Two outfit changes, minimal travel, done by lunch.

Day 2 — Valley & Falls Day (Full Day)

Early start to Dassam or Hundru Falls for morning light, lunch en route, then Patratu Valley from 3 PM — the viewpoint first, then the dam ghats for the golden-hour finale. Three outfits, one big cinematic day.

The Overnight Upgrade

Add Netarhat with a one-night stay for sunrise-point and pine-forest frames no one else in your circle will have. Best combined with an anniversary or post-engagement trip.

Outfit Planning by Location

  • ✓ Patratu Valley: flowing gowns and sarees in reds, whites or cobalt — bold solids pop against the blue-green valley
  • ✓ Waterfalls: lighter fabrics that handle mist, and footwear you can climb rocks in; carry a change
  • ✓ Tagore Hill & heritage spots: traditional wear — sherwani-lehenga combinations photograph beautifully against stone
  • ✓ Gardens & lake: pastels and coordinated casuals for the light, candid frames
  • ✓ Golden rule: maximum 3 outfits per day — more changes cost you your best light hours

Drone, Props & Add-Ons Worth Considering

Patratu's winding road and Getalsud's open water are made for drone shots — budget ₹8,000-15,000 extra and confirm your photographer carries permissions. Small props travel well: a picnic setup, fairy-light jar frames for dusk, sunglasses and hats for candids. Skip heavy props at waterfall locations — carrying them down the steps is misery.
