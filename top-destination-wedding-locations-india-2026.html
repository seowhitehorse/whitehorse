Top Destination Wedding Locations in India (2026)

From royal palaces in Udaipur to hidden hill stations in Jharkhand, here are India's most unforgettable places to say "I do"

By White Horse Event Management - Best Event Management Company in Ranchi • August 2026 • 12 min read

India is arguably the world's greatest destination wedding country. Within one border you can choose between royal palaces, tropical beaches, backwater houseboats, Himalayan foothills, wildlife reserves, and misty hill stations, each offering a completely different wedding experience.

But that abundance is also the challenge: with so many stunning options, which destination is right for your wedding, your guest list, and your budget?

As destination wedding planners who have coordinated celebrations across India from our base in Ranchi, White Horse Event Management has put together this honest, experience-based guide to India's top destination wedding locations for 2026, including what each destination truly costs, when to book, and which type of couple each one suits best.

India's Best Destination Wedding Locations for 2026

1. Udaipur, Rajasthan — The Royal Classic

The City of Lakes remains India's most iconic wedding destination. Palace hotels on Lake Pichola, regal processions, and sunset boat rides give weddings here a cinematic, royal quality that no other city matches.

Best For: Grand, luxury weddings; couples who want the "royal wedding" experience

Best Season: October to March

Watch Out For: Premium pricing and venues booking out 12-18 months in advance for peak dates

2. Jaipur, Rajasthan — Heritage With Connectivity

The Pink City offers heritage forts, palace lawns, and haveli venues with far better flight connectivity than most heritage destinations, which makes guest logistics noticeably easier than in more remote royal towns.

Best For: Large guest lists arriving from multiple cities; fort and palace weddings on a relatively flexible budget

Best Season: October to March

Watch Out For: Summer heat makes April-June outdoor functions difficult

3. Goa — Beach Celebrations & Relaxed Vibes

Goa is the go-to for couples who want their wedding to feel like a holiday: barefoot beach ceremonies, sundowner cocktail parties, and resorts that can host every function on one property.

Best For: Fun-loving couples, younger guest lists, 2-3 day resort weddings

Best Season: November to February

Watch Out For: Monsoon (June-September) rules out beach functions; peak December dates are very expensive

4. Kerala — Backwaters, Greenery & Serenity

Kumarakom, Alleppey, and Kochi offer lush backwater resorts, houseboat experiences, and a naturally elegant setting. Kerala weddings tend to feel intimate, green, and calm rather than loud and grand.

Best For: Intimate weddings, nature-loving couples, wellness-themed celebrations

Best Season: September to March

Watch Out For: Humidity; decor and outfits should be planned for it

5. Jim Corbett, Uttarakhand — Jungle Luxury

Riverside resorts on the edge of India's most famous tiger reserve have become hugely popular for weddings that combine luxury with wilderness: think mandaps by the Kosi river and safari mornings for guests.

Best For: Couples from Delhi-NCR, nature + luxury seekers, 100-250 guest weddings

Best Season: October to April

Watch Out For: Limited premium inventory; the best resorts book out early for winter weekends

6. Rishikesh, Uttarakhand — Spiritual & Scenic

For couples who want the Ganga, the Himalayas, and a spiritual undertone to their celebrations, Rishikesh delivers riverside ceremonies and Ganga aarti experiences guests never forget.

Best For: Spiritual couples, intimate weddings, adventure-loving guest lists (rafting!)

Best Season: October to April

Watch Out For: Alcohol and non-veg restrictions in many areas; plan menus accordingly

7. Netarhat & Patratu Valley, Jharkhand — The Hidden Gem

Here's the destination most lists miss. Jharkhand's Netarhat hill station ("Queen of Chotanagpur") and the lakeside Patratu Valley offer mountain sunrises, pine forests, and waterfront celebrations at a fraction of the cost of Rajasthan or Goa, without the crowds of commercial wedding hubs.

Best For: Couples from Eastern India, value-conscious families who still want breathtaking backdrops, intimate to mid-size weddings

Best Season: October to March

Getting There: Ranchi's Birsa Munda Airport, then a scenic 1.5-4 hour drive depending on the venue

8. Mussoorie, Uttarakhand — Colonial Hill Charm

The Queen of Hills offers colonial-era hotels, valley views, and cool weather, ideal for couples who want a cozy mountain wedding with old-world elegance.

Best For: Small to mid-size weddings, winter wedding lovers

Best Season: April to June, September to November

Watch Out For: Hill logistics: transporting decor and guests takes extra planning

9. Andaman Islands — Barefoot Luxury

For truly intimate celebrations, Havelock (Swaraj Dweep) offers white-sand beaches and turquoise water that rival international island destinations, while keeping the wedding within India.

Best For: Very intimate weddings (under 80 guests), elopement-style celebrations, honeymoon-plus-wedding trips

Best Season: November to April

Watch Out For: Flight logistics and limited vendor availability; almost everything must be planned in advance

10. Worth a Look: Agra, Shimla & Pushkar

  • • Agra: Hotels with Taj Mahal views make for once-in-a-lifetime wedding photography.
  • • Shimla: Classic colonial hill station charm with good hotel inventory.
  • • Pushkar: Boho-desert weddings with camels, dunes, and a laid-back artistic vibe.

How to Choose Your Destination: 5 Questions to Ask

1. How will guests get there?

Count the flights, trains, and road hours from where most of your guests live. A stunning venue loses its shine if half your family arrives exhausted.

2. What does the season look like on your date?

Monsoon in Goa, summer in Rajasthan, and winter fog in the north can each derail outdoor plans. Match the destination to your date, not the other way around.

3. Can the destination host ALL your functions?

Check that the venue (or cluster of venues) can handle Haldi, Mehndi, Sangeet, wedding, and reception without guests shuttling long distances.

4. What's the realistic all-in budget?

Add venue, rooms, travel, F&B, decor, and vendor travel costs. Offbeat destinations like Jharkhand often come out 30-40% cheaper for the same experience level.

5. Is there local planning support?

A planner with real local vendor relationships at your destination will save you more than their fee, in both money and stress.

Destination Wedding Locations in India

Which is the best destination wedding location in India?

+

It depends on your style and budget: Udaipur for royal grandeur, Goa for beach celebrations, Kerala for serenity, and Netarhat or Patratu Valley in Jharkhand for scenic beauty at exceptional value.

Which destination wedding location is the most budget-friendly?

+

Offbeat destinations like Jharkhand's Netarhat and Patratu Valley typically cost 30-40% less than Rajasthan or Goa for a comparable experience, with venues, stay, and vendors all more affordable.

What is the best month for a destination wedding in India?

+

October to March is ideal for most Indian destinations, with pleasant weather for outdoor functions. Hill stations like Mussoorie also work well in April-June.

How far in advance should I book a destination wedding venue?

+

For popular destinations like Udaipur and Goa, book 12-18 months ahead for peak winter dates. Offbeat destinations usually need 6-9 months.
