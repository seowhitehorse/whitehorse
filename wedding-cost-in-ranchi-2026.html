How Much Does a Wedding Cost in Ranchi? A Complete 2026 Price Breakdown

Real 2026 price ranges for every part of your wedding, plus expert tips to save money without cutting corners

By White Horse Event Management, Best Event Management Company in Ranchi • July 2026 • 10 min read

One of the very first questions every couple and family asks is simple: how much does a wedding actually cost in Ranchi? The honest answer is that it depends on your guest count, your venue, and the experience you want to create. A beautiful, well-managed wedding in Ranchi can be planned across a wide range of budgets, from intimate celebrations to grand five-star affairs.

As Ranchi's leading wedding planning and event management company, White Horse Event Management has planned weddings across every budget level in the city and across Jharkhand. In this 2026 guide, we break down realistic costs for each part of your wedding so you can plan with clarity and confidence.

Quick Answer: Wedding Cost in Ranchi (2026)

  • • Budget wedding (150 to 250 guests): approximately ₹5 lakh to ₹12 lakh
  • • Mid-range wedding (300 to 500 guests): approximately ₹15 lakh to ₹30 lakh
  • • Luxury wedding (500 plus guests, 5-star venue): ₹35 lakh and above
  • • Catering is usually the single largest cost, followed by venue and decor

1. Wedding Venue Cost in Ranchi

Your venue is the foundation of your wedding budget because it also influences catering, decor, and guest experience. Ranchi offers everything from affordable community halls to five-star hotels.

  • • Community halls and small banquet halls: ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000 (hall rental)
  • • Mid-range banquet halls and lawns: ₹1,50,000 to ₹4,00,000
  • • Premium hotels and resorts: often priced per plate rather than a flat rental

For a detailed list of options, see our guide to the best wedding venues in Ranchi and Jharkhand.

2. Catering Cost Per Plate in Ranchi

Catering is usually the biggest part of any wedding budget because it is multiplied by your guest count. In Ranchi, per plate costs in 2026 typically look like this:

  • • Standard vegetarian menu: ₹500 to ₹900 per plate
  • • Premium multi-cuisine menu: ₹1,000 to ₹1,800 per plate
  • • Five-star hotel catering: ₹2,000 to ₹3,500 per plate

A 400-guest wedding at ₹1,000 per plate means roughly ₹4 lakh on catering alone. Read our full guide to the best catering services for weddings in Ranchi.

3. Wedding Decoration Cost in Ranchi

Decor sets the mood and appears in every photograph, so it is worth investing in thoughtfully. Depending on the scale, flowers, lighting, and stage design:

  • • Simple, elegant decor: ₹75,000 to ₹2,00,000
  • • Mid-range themed decor: ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,00,000
  • • Luxury floral and lighting setups: ₹5,00,000 and above

See the latest ideas in our luxury wedding decoration trends for 2026.

4. Photography, Entertainment, and Other Costs

  • • Photography and videography: ₹75,000 to ₹3,00,000
  • • DJ, sound, and entertainment: ₹40,000 to ₹1,50,000
  • • Makeup and bridal styling: ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000
  • • Invitations, gifts, and miscellaneous: ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000

Pre-wedding functions like sangeet, mehndi, and haldi add their own costs. See our guide to pre-wedding events in Ranchi.

  • • Venue: ₹2,50,000
  • • Catering (400 x ₹1,000): ₹4,00,000
  • • Decor: ₹3,00,000

This is an indicative example. Your actual cost depends on your choices, and a good planner helps you get the most value from every rupee.

Expert Tips to Save Money on Your Wedding in Ranchi

  • • Book your venue and vendors 4 to 6 months early to lock in better rates
  • • Choose an off-season or weekday date for lower venue and catering prices
  • • Finalize your guest list carefully, since catering scales directly with numbers
  • • Combine functions at one venue to save on setup and travel
  • • Hire a wedding planner, whose vendor network and negotiation often offsets the fee

How much does an average wedding cost in Ranchi?

+

A mid-range wedding in Ranchi for 300 to 500 guests typically costs between ₹15 lakh and ₹30 lakh, while budget weddings start around ₹5 lakh and luxury weddings go well above ₹35 lakh.

What is the catering cost per plate in Ranchi?

+

Wedding catering in Ranchi ranges from ₹500 to ₹900 per plate for standard menus, ₹1,000 to ₹1,800 for premium multi-cuisine menus, and ₹2,000 to ₹3,500 at five-star hotels.

Which part of the wedding costs the most?

+

Catering is usually the single largest expense because it multiplies with your guest count, followed by venue and decoration.

How can I reduce my wedding cost in Ranchi?

+

Book early, choose an off-season or weekday date, control your guest list, combine functions at one venue, and use a planner whose vendor network gives you better rates.

How much does wedding decoration cost in Ranchi?

+

Wedding decor in Ranchi ranges from ₹75,000 for simple, elegant setups to ₹5 lakh and above for luxury floral and lighting installations, depending on scale and theme.
