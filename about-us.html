Our Story

Born in Ranchi, Built on Trust

At White Horse Event Management, every celebration is an expression of emotion, elegance and excellence. We believe weddings are not just events but once-in-a-lifetime stories woven with love, culture and dreams, and that a corporate event is an investment in relationships, reputation and growth.

We started with a simple conviction: celebrations in Jharkhand deserved the same design thinking and execution discipline found in India's metro cities, delivered by a team that lives here and knows the venues, vendors, seasons and traditions first-hand.

Over 10+ years that conviction has grown into a company with more than 500 events behind it, a head office on Mahatma Gandhi Main Road in Ranchi, a second office in Bistupur, Jamshedpur, and destination weddings planned for Jharkhand families across India.
