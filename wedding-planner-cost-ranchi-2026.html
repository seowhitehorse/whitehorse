How Much Does a Wedding Planner Cost in Ranchi? (2026 Pricing Guide)

A transparent breakdown of what wedding planners charge in Ranchi, what is included, and how to assess whether it is worth it

By White Horse Event Management — Best Event Management Company in Ranchi • September 2026 • 9 min read

One of the questions we get most often at White Horse Event Management is: "What do you charge?" It is a fair question, and it deserves a direct, honest answer — which is why we wrote this guide.

Wedding planner pricing in Ranchi is not standardised. Rates vary based on the type of service, the scale of the wedding, the experience of the company, and what is actually included in the package. This guide breaks it all down so you can compare options and make an informed decision.

Quick Answer: Wedding Planner Cost in Ranchi (2026)

  • • Day-of coordination only: ₹25,000 to ₹60,000
  • • Partial planning (final 2–3 months): ₹50,000 to ₹1,20,000
  • • Full wedding management: ₹1,20,000 to ₹3,50,000+
  • • Percentage-of-budget model: some planners charge 8–12% of total wedding budget

The Three Types of Wedding Planning Services

Understanding the service type is more important than the number itself. The same price can mean very different things depending on what is included.

1. Day-of Coordination

You plan everything yourself. The coordinator takes over 2–4 weeks before the wedding to confirm vendors, build a run-of-day schedule, and manage the wedding day itself so you are not fielding calls while getting dressed.

Best for: Couples who want to plan their own wedding but need expert hands on the actual day.

2. Partial Planning

You handle the early decisions (venue, broad budget, guest list). The planner steps in at the 2–3 month mark to source remaining vendors, manage timelines, and handle day-of coordination.

Best for: Couples who have some things booked but want professional support for the detail and execution phase.

3. Full Wedding Management

The planner is involved from start to finish. They help set the budget, select and negotiate with vendors, manage all pre-wedding events, and handle the entire wedding-day operation. You make the decisions; they execute everything.

Best for: Busy couples, families without local contacts, destination weddings, or anyone who wants the entire process managed professionally.

What Affects Wedding Planner Pricing in Ranchi

  • Wedding scale: A 100-guest wedding requires less coordination than a 600-guest multi-day event.
  • Number of events: Managing only the main wedding versus the full sequence of engagement, sangeet, mehndi, haldi, and wedding adds complexity and cost.
  • Destination component: If any events are outside Ranchi, travel and logistics management adds to the scope.
  • Vendor sourcing: Planners with strong vendor networks can often secure better rates than you would get independently.
  • Timeline: Shorter planning timelines require more intensive management and may cost more.

Does a Wedding Planner Actually Save You Money?

This is the real question. The honest answer is: usually yes, but the way they save you money is often indirect.

Vendor Negotiation

Experienced planners have long-term relationships with caterers, decorators, and venues. That often translates into better rates and terms than a first-time client would get.

Preventing Costly Mistakes

Missed clauses in contracts, forgotten deposits, last-minute vendor cancellations — these events cost far more than a planner's fee.

Budget Discipline

A good planner keeps your spending on track, flags when a category is running over, and suggests adjustments before costs spiral.

Time Has Value Too

The hours you spend researching caterers, visiting venues, and chasing confirmations are real hours. A planner's fee is also buying back your time.

Questions to Ask Before Hiring a Wedding Planner in Ranchi

  • • What exactly is included in your fee? What is extra?
  • • How many weddings do you manage at the same time?
  • • Who will be our primary contact, and who handles the wedding day?
  • • Can we see references or speak to past clients?
  • • How do you handle vendor disputes or day-of emergencies?
  • • Do you earn commissions from vendors you recommend?
