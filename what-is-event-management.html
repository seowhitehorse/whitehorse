What is Event Management? Process, Types & Why It Matters

A plain-language guide to how professional events are planned and delivered, from a team that has run more than 500 of them

Event management is the planning, organisation and on-the-day execution of an event, whether that is a wedding for 800 guests, a product launch for 200 dealers or a birthday party for 40 children. It covers everything from the first budget conversation to the last vendor payment.

Most people meet event management only when they are about to host something big and realise how many moving parts are involved. This guide explains what the discipline includes, how the process works in practice, the different types of events it covers, and how to decide whether you need a professional.

Event Management Definition

Event management is the application of project management to the creation of an event. An event manager takes an objective (celebrate a marriage, launch a product, honour employees), a budget and a date, and is responsible for every decision and vendor required to deliver it successfully.

The key word is responsible. A decorator is responsible for decor. A caterer is responsible for food. An event manager is responsible for the whole event working together, on time, within budget.

Event Planning vs. Event Management

The terms are often used interchangeably, but there is a useful distinction:

  • • Event planning is the "before": concept, budget, venue, vendor selection, design and schedules.
  • • Event management includes planning and the "during": production, logistics, crew, timeline control and problem-solving on the day.
  • • Event coordination is sometimes offered on its own: a team that runs an event you have already planned.

A full-service event management company does all three. Read our guide on why hiring a professional event planner is worth it for the practical trade-offs.

The Event Management Process: 5 Stages

1. Research & objectives

What is the event for, who is coming, what must happen, and what does success look like? For a wedding this is families, traditions and guest count. For a corporate event it is audience, message and business outcome.

2. Design & concept

Theme, venue type, decor direction, food style, entertainment and guest experience are decided and turned into a visual concept and a budget.

3. Planning & procurement

Venue booking, vendor contracts, floor plans, run-of-show timelines, guest logistics, permissions and insurance. This is the longest stage.

4. Coordination & execution

Setup, rehearsals, vendor management, timeline control and live problem-solving on the event day(s).

5. Evaluation & settlement

Vendor settlements, venue handover, final accounts against the budget, and a review of what to improve next time.

From Our Experience

Almost every event problem traces back to stage 3. A missing generator, a caterer who assumed a different headcount, a stage built to the wrong dimensions: these are planning failures that show up on the day. The quality of an event management company is measured by how boring its event days are.

Types of Event Management

Social & wedding events

Weddings, receptions, engagements, sangeet, mehndi, haldi, birthdays, anniversaries and religious functions. In India this is the largest segment of the industry. See our wedding planning services.

Corporate events

Conferences, product launches, annual days, dealer and distributor meets, award nights, team offsites and CSR programmes. Read about corporate event management.

Destination events

Weddings and offsites held away from the host's home city, adding travel, accommodation and multi-day hospitality to the scope. See destination wedding planning in India.

Public & cultural events

Festivals, concerts, exhibitions, sporting events and government functions, with permissions, crowd management and security as major components.

Virtual & hybrid events

Online or part-online events with streaming, remote audience engagement and technical production.

What Event Management Services Include

  • ✓ Budgeting & financial control — allocating money across venue, food, decor, production and contingency, and tracking it
  • ✓ Venue sourcing & negotiation — shortlisting, site visits, contracts and layouts
  • ✓ Vendor management — caterers, decorators, photographers, sound and light, entertainers, transport
  • ✓ Design & decor — theme, stage, lighting, florals and signage
  • ✓ Guest management — invitations, RSVPs, hotel blocks, transport and hospitality desks
  • ✓ Production — stage, sound, LED walls, lighting, power and technical crew
  • ✓ Permissions & compliance — venue rules, local permissions, safety and insurance
  • ✓ On-ground execution — coordinators and crew running the timeline on the day

Skills a Good Event Manager Needs

Budgeting, negotiation, design sense, logistics planning, vendor relationships, calm under pressure and the ability to make decisions quickly with incomplete information. The last two matter most on event day. Experience is the only real teacher, which is why the number and scale of events a company has delivered is the first thing to ask about.

When Should You Hire an Event Management Company?

You can self-manage a small gathering. Consider a professional when any of the following is true:

  • ✓ More than one function or more than one day
  • ✓ More than 150 guests, or guests travelling from other cities
  • ✓ Six or more vendors who need to work in sync
  • ✓ A business outcome depends on the event (launch, client meet, brand event)
  • ✓ You want to be a host, not a supervisor, on the day

Event Management Basics

What is event management in simple words?

+

Event management is planning, organising and running an event from start to finish: budget, venue, vendors, design, guest logistics and on-the-day execution, under one responsible team.

What are the 5 stages of event management?

+

Research and objectives, design and concept, planning and procurement, coordination and execution, and evaluation and settlement.

What is the difference between event planning and event management?

+

Event planning covers the preparation before an event. Event management includes planning plus production and execution on the day. A full-service company does both.

What types of events do event management companies handle?

+

Weddings and social functions, corporate events such as conferences and launches, destination events, public and cultural events, and virtual or hybrid events.

What services are included in event management?

+

Budgeting, venue sourcing, vendor management, design and decor, guest management, production, permissions and on-ground execution.

When should I hire an event management company?
