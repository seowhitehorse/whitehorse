Wedding Photography & Videography in Ranchi: A 2026 Guide with Prices

Compare candid photography, cinematic films, drone coverage, and pre-wedding shoots by style and budget

Long after the flowers fade and the food is finished, your photographs and wedding film are what remain. Choosing the right wedding photographer and videographer in Ranchi is one of the most emotional and most confusing decisions couples face, because portfolios all look beautiful and prices vary enormously.

Wedding Photography Prices in Ranchi at a Glance (2026)

  • • Traditional photography and video: ₹30,000 to ₹80,000 per day
  • • Candid photography plus cinematic film packages: ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000
  • • Premium teams with drone, same-day edits, and albums: ₹2,50,000 to ₹6,00,000+
  • • Pre-wedding shoots: ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 depending on location and team
  • • Top teams get booked 6 to 9 months in advance for peak dates

1. Traditional Photography and Videography

Traditional coverage focuses on posed portraits, family group photos, and complete documentation of every ritual. It remains popular for Ranchi weddings because parents and elders value formal family photographs and full-length ceremony videos.

What you get: Posed portraits, ritual coverage, full event video, printed albums

Indicative price: ₹30,000 to ₹80,000 per day

Best for: Families who want complete, formal coverage of every function at a controlled budget.

2. Candid Photography and Cinematic Films

Candid photographers capture natural, unposed moments: the laughter during haldi, tears during vidaai, and stolen glances during the pheras. Paired with a cinematic wedding film, this style produces the emotional, storytelling coverage most couples now want.

What you get: Candid stills, cinematic highlight film (3 to 5 minutes), longer feature film, teaser for social media

Indicative price: ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000 for a two to three day wedding

Best for: Couples who want natural, emotional storytelling alongside traditional family coverage.

3. Premium Packages: Drone, Same-Day Edits, and Luxury Albums

For grand weddings and destination celebrations, premium teams add aerial drone coverage, same-day edit films screened at the reception, multiple photographer and cinematographer crews, and imported fine-art albums. These teams often travel from metros or are Ranchi's most in-demand studios.

What you get: Multi-crew coverage, drone shots, same-day edits, luxury albums, dedicated editing team

Indicative price: ₹2,50,000 to ₹6,00,000 and above

Best for: Luxury weddings, destination weddings, and couples who want magazine-quality coverage.

4. Pre-Wedding Shoots in and Around Ranchi

Pre-wedding shoots have become a must-have, and Ranchi offers stunning natural backdrops within a short drive: Patratu Valley's winding roads and lake, Rock Garden and Kanke Dam, Hundru and Jonha Falls, and the pine forests around Netarhat. Many couples also choose styled indoor sets and cafes.

Popular locations: Patratu Valley, Kanke Dam, Rock Garden, Hundru Falls, Netarhat

Indicative price: ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 including team, travel, and edited output

Best for: Couples who want relaxed, styled portraits before the wedding rush begins.

How to Choose a Wedding Photographer in Ranchi

  • • Watch full wedding films and complete albums, not just Instagram highlights
  • • Confirm who will actually shoot your wedding, the lead photographer or a junior team
  • • Check deliverables in writing: number of edited photos, film lengths, album pages
  • • Ask about delivery timelines, good teams commit to 6 to 10 weeks for final output
  • • Confirm backup equipment, extra batteries, and data backup practices
  • • Understand the payment schedule and what happens if dates change

Frequently Asked Questions

Wedding Photography in Ranchi

How much does wedding photography cost in Ranchi?

+

Traditional coverage starts around ₹30,000 to ₹80,000 per day, candid plus cinematic packages run ₹80,000 to ₹2,50,000, and premium multi-crew teams with drone and same-day edits cost ₹2,50,000 to ₹6,00,000 or more.

What is the difference between candid and traditional wedding photography?

+

Traditional photography focuses on posed portraits and formal family group photos, while candid photography captures natural, unposed moments and emotions. Most couples now book both styles together.

How early should I book a wedding photographer in Ranchi?

+

Book 6 to 9 months in advance for peak season dates between October and February. The most in-demand candid teams in Ranchi are often fully booked for the season by early autumn.

How much does a pre-wedding shoot cost in Ranchi?

+

Pre-wedding shoots in and around Ranchi typically cost ₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 depending on the team, location, styling, and number of outfits and edited photos included.

Which are the best pre-wedding shoot locations near Ranchi?

+

Patratu Valley, Kanke Dam, Rock Garden, Hundru Falls, Jonha Falls, and Netarhat are the most popular natural locations. Styled indoor sets and cafes in the city are also common choices.

Is drone coverage allowed at weddings in Ranchi?

+

Yes, drone coverage is common at Ranchi weddings, though some venues and areas have restrictions. Your photography team or planner confirms venue permission and handles compliance before the event.

How long does it take to get wedding photos and films delivered?

+

Good teams deliver a teaser within a week, edited photos in 4 to 8 weeks, and the full film and album in 6 to 12 weeks. Always get the delivery timeline in writing before booking.

What is a same-day edit wedding film?

+

A same-day edit is a short film cut from the morning's ceremonies and screened at the evening reception. It is a premium add-on that needs an extra editor on site and is a highlight guests love.

Should I book photographers from Ranchi or from a metro city?

+

Ranchi now has excellent candid and cinematic teams at better prices than metro studios, which charge extra for travel and stay. Metro teams make sense mainly for very large luxury weddings.
