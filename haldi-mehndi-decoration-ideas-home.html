Haldi & Mehndi Decoration Ideas at Home

Marigold magic, budget-friendly backdrops and setups that make home functions look designer-done

By White Horse Events • September 2026 • 9 min read

Haldi and mehndi are the warmest functions of the wedding — and they happen mostly at home, in courtyards, rooftops and living rooms. The good news: with the right colours and a few smart setups, home functions can look absolutely designer-done on a modest budget. Here are the ideas our decor team uses most. (Planning the functions themselves? See our full sangeet, mehndi & haldi planning guide.)

The Foundation: Genda Phool (Marigold) Everything

Marigold is cheap, locally available year-round, and photographs better than flowers ten times its price. The classic haldi palette is marigold + yellow drapes + banana leaves; for mehndi, add greens, pinks and mirror-work elements.

  • ✓ Marigold strings backdrop: 30-50 strings hung dense on a bamboo frame — the single highest-impact, lowest-cost setup
  • ✓ Phoolon ki chadar: a marigold canopy over the haldi seat turns any courtyard corner into the function's centrepiece
  • ✓ Urli bowls: brass or steel bowls with floating marigolds and candles along walkways and staircases
  • ✓ Banana leaves + marigold: the traditional combination that instantly says 'haldi' in photos

Setups That Make the Function

The Haldi Seat

A low wooden chowki or moodha under the marigold canopy, yellow cushions, and a rangoli ring around it. Keep 8-10 feet clear around the seat so everyone can gather and the photographer can move.

The Mehndi Lounge

Low seating — mattresses, bolsters and mirror-work cushions on durries — with hanging bangles, colourful parasols or dupattas overhead. Artists need good light: plan the seating where daylight falls, or add warm bulbs.

The Photo Corner

One dedicated selfie backdrop — marigold wall, 'Haldi' or couple-name cutout, and a few props (sunglasses, phoolon ka sehra). Guests use it all evening and your feed fills itself.

The Small Details

Yellow dupattas as table runners, kaleere and bangles as hangings, fairy lights wound through everything after sunset.

DIY vs Professional: What It Costs

  • • Full DIY (family + local flower market): ₹8,000 – ₹15,000 — best for small courtyard functions
  • • Professional basic package: ₹15,000 – ₹30,000 — backdrop, canopy, seat setup and lighting done for you
  • • Professional premium package: ₹30,000 – ₹75,000 — full-home decor, mehndi lounge, photo corner and after-dark lighting

The honest tradeoff: DIY saves money but costs the family the entire function morning. A professional team sets up while your family gets ready.

One Mistake to Avoid

Don't spread the budget thin across the whole house. Concentrate 80% of the decor on the two spots where photos actually happen — the haldi/mehndi seat and the photo corner — and keep the rest simple with fairy lights.

Colour Palettes That Photograph Beautifully

Classic Haldi Yellow

Marigold + yellow drapes + banana leaves + white accents. Foolproof, traditional, and the turmeric moments glow against it. Add copper/brass vessels for texture.

Pastel Haldi

Soft yellows with white and blush florals, cane furniture and sheer drapes — the Instagram-era haldi that still feels traditional. Costs slightly more (mixed flowers) but photographs like a magazine spread.

Jewel-Tone Mehndi

Emerald, magenta and orange with mirror-work cushions, colourful parasols and bangles strung as hangings. Evening mehndi functions in this palette come alive under warm string lights.

Lighting: The Difference Maker

Decor budgets obsess over flowers, but after sunset the lighting IS the decor. Warm (2700-3000K) string lights always — never white LED strips, which make skin tones and marigold look flat in photos.

  • ✓ Fairy-light curtain behind the backdrop doubles its depth in photos — ₹1,500-3,000
  • ✓ Festoon/bulb strings zigzagged over the courtyard for the ceiling — ₹2,000-5,000
  • ✓ Diyas and candles in urlis at floor level for the golden glow layer
  • ✓ One focused warm light on the haldi seat so every ritual photo is well-lit
  • ✓ Avoid: colour-changing disco LEDs during rituals — save them for the dance hour

Setup Timeline & DIY Shopping List

Professional teams set up the evening before (functions before noon) or from 6 AM (afternoon functions). DIY families should finish structural work — frames, drapes, lights — the night before, keeping only fresh-flower work for the morning: marigold wilts in 12-18 hours unstrung.

  • • Marigold: 40-60 strings for a full backdrop + canopy (₹2,500-5,000 at Ranchi flower market rates)
  • • Yellow drapes/dupattas: 8-10 pieces — buy plain fabric by the metre, far cheaper than readymade
  • • String lights: 4-6 warm fairy strings + 2 festoon strings
  • • Base & seating: chowki, durries, 12-15 cushion covers, 2-3 low tables
  • • Extras: urlis or steel bowls, candles, rangoli colours, banana leaves, bangles for hangings

Total DIY spend lands ₹8,000-15,000 as promised — and everything except the flowers gets reused at the engagement and reception.

Haldi & Mehndi Decoration

How much does haldi decoration at home cost?

+

DIY with local flowers costs ₹8,000-15,000; professional packages run ₹15,000-30,000 for the essentials and ₹30,000-75,000 for full-home premium decor.

Which flowers are best for haldi decoration?

+

Marigold (genda phool) is the classic choice — affordable, available year-round and beautiful in photos. Pair it with yellow drapes and banana leaves for the traditional look.

What is the most important part of haldi decor?

+

The haldi seat setup — a decorated chowki under a marigold canopy — and one good photo backdrop. Concentrate your budget on these two spots.

How should we set up a mehndi function at home?

+

Low floor seating with cushions and durries, good natural light for the mehndi artists, hanging bangles or parasols overhead, and a photo corner for guests.

What lighting should we use for a home haldi function?

+

Warm (2700-3000K) fairy and festoon lights only — never white LED strips. Add diyas in urlis at floor level and one focused warm light on the haldi seat for ritual photos.

When should we set up the decoration?

+

Finish frames, drapes and lights the night before; do fresh marigold work the same morning — unstrung marigold wilts within 12-18 hours.

Which colour theme is trending for mehndi functions?

+

Jewel tones — emerald, magenta and orange with mirror-work cushions and parasols — for evening functions, and pastel yellows with cane furniture for daytime ones.
